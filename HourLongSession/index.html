Hour Long Session

This is a shorter version of my in-depth, two-and-a-half-hour healing session.

You'll begin by getting comfortable on my healing table, where I'll gently clear your aura using sage that I've harvested from my garden—grounding your energy and creating a sacred, cleansed space for healing.

In this one-hour session, we'll start by checking your chakras to see which energy centers need the most support. I'll place crystals intuitively on your body to help bring balance and alignment, then activate your chakras using my master quartz crystal—infusing each one with high-frequency energy.

You'll then receive a 15-minute sound bath, focused on the specific chakras we're working with. The vibrational medicine of the Crystal Alchemy Singing Bowls helps the body and energy field gently tune to harmonic frequencies, like re-stringing the instrument of your soul. Sometimes I'm also guided to sing or add other instruments based on the energy present.

Following the sound bath, I'll begin the Reiki portion of the session. For this shorter session, Reiki will be given with you lying face up only, and I'll focus especially on the chakras or areas of the body that need extra attention. Calming music will play softly in the background to support relaxation, and I may continue to incorporate the sound bowls as needed to enhance energetic flow.

I also use essential oils that correspond with the chakras to deepen the healing, bringing in the subtle support of plant medicine. While I often use tuning forks in my extended sessions, they may or may not be included in this shorter format depending on the needs of the session.

As we come to a close, I will gently seal your chakras and comb through your aura with my master crystal to release any lingering stagnant energy and strengthen your energetic field—clearing away what no longer serves you.

To complete the session, I'll play my custom 34-inch gong from Grotta Sonora in Italy, allowing its deep, resonant tones to integrate and ground the healing.

I'll then gently guide you out of the meditative state, and we'll have a conversation about how you're feeling, what I observed during the session, and which crystals were used. I'll also share practices—such as meditations, breathwork, or visualizations—that you can use to bring more energy to any chakras that may need continued support, along with any intuitive or spiritual guidance that came through.

The healing portion of this session is approximately 45 minutes, with the final 15 minutes reserved for integration, sharing, and spiritual support.

Consecutive Session Benefits:
When you book multiple sessions close together, you create deeper layers of healing and transformation. Each session builds upon the previous one, allowing us to work more deeply with your energy centers, clear long-held patterns, and integrate powerful breathwork techniques that become tools you can use at home. This progressive approach helps establish lasting energetic shifts and gives you a comprehensive toolkit for ongoing self-care and spiritual growth.
