✨ What Is Reiki?

Reiki is a Japanese energy healing technique that channels life force energy through the hands to promote relaxation, balance, and healing. It works by gently dissolving energetic blocks and supporting the body's natural ability to heal. Reiki isn't tied to any one belief system—it simply works with your energy field to restore harmony on a physical, emotional, mental, and spiritual level. I am a certified Reiki Master, and in our sessions I use both traditional symbols and intuitive guidance to bring energy where it's most needed.
